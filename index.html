Sectors of Impact

Tailored for Industries.

Delivering specialized compliance, high-tolerance metrology, and operational transformation across the world's most demanding sectors.

Oil & Gas

API Spec Q1/Q2 implementation, Monogram license assistance, casing & tubing inspection, hydrostatic testing, and vendor assessments for upstream, midstream, and offshore platforms.

Automotive

IATF 16949:2016 alignment, VDA 6.3 process auditing, core tools deployment (APQP, FMEA, MSA, SPC, PPAP), and high-precision gauge calibration for tier-1 automotive suppliers.

Aerospace & Defense

AS 9100 Rev D quality systems, flight-critical component layout inspections, calibration traceability, and Nadcap-aligned process controls for precision aerospace manufacturers.

Manufacturing

Comprehensive Total Productive Maintenance (TPM), 5S/6S workplace organization, Lean Six Sigma DMAIC workflows, and on-site annual calibration contracts (AMC).

Healthcare & Process

Biomedical instrumentation calibration, Clean Room validation conforming to ISO 14644, thermal chamber mapping, HVAC qualification, and gas analyzer verification.

Pharma

WHO-GMP & US-FDA 21 CFR Part 11 validation, autoclave & stability chamber thermal mapping, cleanroom ISO 14644 certification, and precision calibration for pharmaceutical formulation units.

Medical

ISO 13485:2016 medical device compliance, biomedical test equipment verification, surgical instrumentation metrology, patient monitor sensor audits, and diagnostic device accuracy.

Food & Beverage

HACCP, ISO 22000 & FSSC compliance, cold chain thermal mapping, packaging line load cells, pasteurizer temperature controllers, and refractometer & pH sensor calibration.
