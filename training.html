Flagship Course

ISO/IEC 17025:2017 • IRCA Accredited Lead Auditor Training

Reva Phoenix Labs & Consultants Pvt. Ltd. is one of India's premier metrology institutions offering an IRCA accredited 5-Day Lead Auditor Training Course. The course is globally recognized and delivered both in-person at our Chennai Corporate Complex and via virtual classroom.

Course Curriculum & 9 Lecture Sessions

This course acknowledges participants with how to conduct opening meetings, perform external audits, assess adequacy of documented information, evaluate measurement uncertainty, and conduct closing meetings conforming to ISO 19011 and ISO/IEC 17025:2017 standards.

Session 01 Awareness & Foundational Principles of ISO/IEC 17025:2017
Session 02 ISO 17025:2017 Laboratory Accreditation Process & Statutory Governance
Session 03 Comprehensive Requirements of Laboratory Management System Clauses
Session 04 Documented Information for ISO/IEC 17025:2017 Implementation
Session 05 Risk-Based Thinking & Opportunity Management in Metrology
Session 06 ISO/IEC 17025:2017 Audit Process Lifecycle & ISO 19011 Guidelines
Session 07 Audit Terms, Definitions, Questioning Techniques & Auditor Roles
Session 08 Performing Document Review & Practical Auditing with Real Laboratory Manuals
Session 09 Nonconformity Reporting, Root Cause Analysis & Corrective Action Verification

Examination & Certification

Participants must clear 9 session evaluations and 1 final examination. Upon passing, the formal IRCA Accredited ISO/IEC 17025:2017 Lead Auditor Certificate is awarded, certifying the candidate for international audit assignments.

Core Competency

Laboratory QMS & Internal Auditor Training • 4 Days

Four-day comprehensive training program on Laboratory Management & Internal Audit as per ISO/IEC 17025:2017, designed specifically for testing & calibration personnel preparing for initial or surveillance NABL assessments.

Key Features of ISO/IEC 17025:2017 Internal Auditor Program

To achieve and maintain NABL accreditation, laboratories must demonstrate that they operate a compliant quality management system, maintain technical competence, and produce technically valid results. This course covers:

Risk-Based Thinking

Incorporating risk identification, opportunities assessment, and performance-based guidelines to prevent calibration nonconformities.

LIMS & Electronic Records

Validating automated data acquisition, Laboratory Information Management Systems (LIMS), and digital certificate integrity.

Measurement Uncertainty

Type A & Type B evaluation, budget preparation, coverage factors, and establishing Calibration & Measurement Capability (CMC).

Audit Execution & Reporting

Formulating internal audit schedules, opening/closing meetings, non-conformance logging, and Management Review documentation.

On-Site Corporate Delivery: In addition to scheduled batches at Reva Phoenix Complex, our instructors conduct customized on-premise Internal Auditor training for corporate client facilities across India and the Middle East.
