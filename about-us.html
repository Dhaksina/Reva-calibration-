Corporate Profile

Technically the Best. Infrastructurally the Largest.

Reva Phoenix is a premier Calibration Laboratory, ISO 9001: 2015 Certified and accredited by NABL for the latest ISO/IEC 17025: 2017 standard requirements for Calibration Laboratories, located in Chennai, India.

Reva Phoenix Labs & Consultants Private Limited is India's Largest Integrated & NABL Accredited Calibration Laboratory; technically, the best and infrastructurally, the largest integrated Calibration Lab with 20+ Metrological Laboratories, all under one roof to serve our customers across India and the Middle East.

Being an all-in-one integrated Calibration Laboratory, we provide Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) as a Single Source facility for all your calibration needs. We provide “at-lab” and “On-site” Calibration services using the best of Calibration Master Equipment. We offer turn-key solutions and services with all calibration laboratories under one roof. We also function as a Nodal Laboratory for Inter-Laboratory Comparison (ILC) with the best Uncertainty / CMC values for most laboratory parameters.

We are also working towards becoming an accredited laboratory for ISO/IEC 17043: 2023 | Conformity assessment – General requirements for the Competence of Proficiency Testing Providers.

Environmentally Controlled Infrastructure

We operate out of a highly sophisticated and state-of-the-art 8,000 square feet facility in Chennai featuring 20+ environmentally controlled laboratories, equipped with high-precision calibration equipment. All reference standards used in calibration are traceable to National / International Standards (NPL, PTB, NIST).

  • Class 10,000 Cleanroom Environments
  • Temperature ± 0.5°C & RH Stabilization
  • 5S Certified by QCFI & AOTS
  • Independent Third-Party Verification

We are the only Laboratory in India certified for 5S, best-in-class Laboratory practices, awarded by Quality Circle Forum of India (QCFI) and honored by ABK-AOTSDOSAKAI.

Our laboratories are staffed with a highly competent and certified team of engineers and technicians who are ready to support you with a wide range of services in all phases of your measurement projects. With our service contracts tailored to your exact needs, you are assured of being on the safe side, whether you require all services or specialized parameters. We conduct regular skill development workshops for our workforce, enabling them to be equipped with the latest software, technology, and equipment installed at our unit.

Laboratory Division Quality Management System (QMS)
Doc ID: RPL-L01-DI-A01-I11 • Effective: 20.09.2024

Reva Phoenix Labs & Consultants Pvt. Ltd. – Laboratory Division is committed to provide & maintain high quality Calibration and excellent professional services to our clients in compliance with Good Laboratory Practices to achieve customer satisfaction by providing Professional, Quality, Timely and Reliable – Calibration services.

01
Impartiality & Confidentiality Safeguards: We are committed to perform our activities impartially and confidentially, in a structured way, and manage to safeguard impartiality and confidentiality. We shall not allow commercial, financial, or other pressures to compromise our policy of impartiality, confidentiality, and our ‘Quality First’ concept.
02
Risk Assessment & Proprietary Security: Risk assessment and identifying opportunities for improvement shall be our way of life in every process, without any impartiality. We are responsible legally for the management of all information obtained or created during our laboratory activities. We shall upkeep all customer information, in any form, as proprietary and confidential.
03
Best-in-Class QMS & Standards Compliance: We are committed to implement Best-in-class Laboratory Quality Management System, per the requirements of ISO 9001: 2015 & ISO/IEC 17025: 2017 international standards; continual improvement and training to all concerned personnel in our laboratory to enhance competency; familiarization and implementation of all four levels of documented information in our day-to-day activities.
04
NABL Guidelines & Statutory Compliance: We are committed to ensure impartiality & confidentiality with high standards and consistent operation in our calibration to our customers in our chosen area of work as per NABL guidelines and requirements. We are committed to perform calibration in accordance with National / International standards / stated methods / customer requirements, complying with applicable statutory and regulatory requirements to achieve total customer satisfaction.
